1. ABOUT NESTIO PRESCHOOLS
- Nestio Preschools is an early childhood education initiative by Space EC India Foundation focused on joyful, experiential, play-based learning.
- Curriculum: Montessori-inspired & NEP 2020 aligned holistic curriculum focusing on cognitive, social, emotional, and motor skill development.
- Safety & Care: 100% CCTV-monitored child-safe campus, hygienic classrooms, trained and caring female educators, and low student-to-teacher ratio.

2. PROGRAMS & AGE ELIGIBILITY
- Playgroup: Age 1.5 to 2.5 years | Timings: 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM (Mon–Fri)
- Nursery: Age 2.5 to 3.5 years | Timings: 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM (Mon–Fri)
- Junior KG (LKG): Age 3.5 to 4.5 years | Timings: 9:00 AM – 12:30 PM (Mon–Fri)
- Senior KG (UKG): Age 4.5 to 5.5 years | Timings: 9:00 AM – 12:30 PM (Mon–Fri)
- Daycare / Extended Care: Available on request (please visit campus for customized timing slots).

3. ADMISSION JOURNEY (4 SIMPLE STEPS)
- Step 1: Enquiry & Program Guidance — Share your child's age to get the right program recommendation and digital brochure.
- Step 2: Campus Tour — Visit the school with your child to meet our educators, inspect classrooms, and experience our play area.
- Step 3: Application Submission — Fill out the admission form and submit required documents.
- Step 4: Seat Confirmation & Enrollment — Complete fee payment to confirm your child's seat and receive the Welcome Kit & Orientation schedule.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR ADMISSION
- Child's Birth Certificate (photocopy)
- Child's Aadhaar Card (if available) & 4 passport-size photographs
- Parents' ID & Address Proof (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport)
- Child's Immunization / Vaccination Record
